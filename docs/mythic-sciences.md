# Mythic Sciences — the tone arc of the Natural Philosophy tree

Working doc (2026-08-23). The game ends ~renaissance/early-industrial: Age IV is the
"future" as a renaissance magister would dream it — brass, aether, clockwork — never
smokestacks. Candidate techs for the honing passes; nothing here is scheduled.

## The two principles

1. **The eastern material is the mainline, not a branch.** Gunpowder (Taoist elixir
   experiments), the compass (the luopan, a feng shui instrument first), movable type
   (Bi Sheng), porcelain, civil examinations — the "mystical eastern" sciences are the
   *actual* history Civ usually westernizes. Told truthfully, they already sound magical.
2. **Real kernels only.** Every tech names something that existed (or was earnestly
   believed); the fantastic register comes from the telling, not from invention. The tree
   shades from myth-that-was-real (Age I) to speculation-that-felt-real (Age IV).

## The long threads (plant early, pay off late)

Each thread is a renewal lineage — later techs renew earlier techs' buildings/improvements
using the building-renewal mechanism, so the narrative spine is also a mechanical one.

- **The Fate-Reading thread** (divination becomes computation):
  Divination (oracle bones, entrails) → Letters (writing *literally* born on oracle bones —
  the Letters←Divination prereq is true history) → The I Ching (fate systematized into
  hexagrams; Leibniz's binary) → The Loom That Remembers (Jacquard punch cards) →
  **The Calculating Engine**. "Reading the will of heaven" ends as computing it.
- **The Fire thread** (kiln to Philosopher's Stone):
  Earthenware (kiln) → Bronzeworking (ritual vessels for the ancestors first, spears second)
  → The First Distillation (alchemy begins) → Fire Medicine (gunpowder from immortality
  experiments) → The White Gold (porcelain) → **The Great Work**.
- **The Sky thread** (ground to heavens):
  Calendar (planting stars) → Stonecraft (megaliths that remember the solstice) →
  The Music of the Spheres → The Astronomical Bureau → The Perspective Glass →
  **The Paper Lantern That Lifted** (aerostats).
- **The Water thread**: river farms → aqueduct → watermill → The Grand Canal.

## Age I — myth that was real (mostly flavor-dress on the shipped tree)

- **Divination**: flavor = oracle bones and entrails; its prereq on Husbandry (herds to
  read) and Letters descending from it are both historically honest — say so in flavor text.
- **Bronzeworking**: ritual vessels first — ding cauldrons for the ancestors; weapons second.
- **Stonecraft**: megaliths as calendars — "stones that remember the solstice"; natural
  monument/Calendar synergy hook for a mastery later.
- **The Hearth** (mastery/flavor register): granary line as hearth-cult — fire kept alive.

## Age II — the learned arts, where early science felt like magic

- **Pneumatica** — Hero of Alexandria's engines: temples whose doors open themselves
  (real staged miracles, 1st c.). Temple renewal (+happiness/culture); the planted seed of
  steam, sixteen centuries early.
- **The Orrery of Bronze** — the Antikythera mechanism: a clockwork astronomical computer
  from ~100 BC. Library/science renewal; foreshadows Clockwork and the Calculating Engine.
- **The Music of the Spheres** — Pythagorean harmonics: number as divine order.
  Science+culture hybrid; amphitheater/temple renewal.
- **The First Distillation** — alchemy begins (Alexandria + Chinese jindan). Market/gold
  renewal; the explicit opening of the ascent that ends at the Great Work.
- **The Body's Rivers** — meridians/acupuncture: units heal faster in friendly territory.
- **The Silk Mystery** — silk as guarded state secret (smuggled eggs, Justinian's monks):
  plantation renewal +gold.
- **Greek Fire** — the lost incendiary formula (Age II/III military, naval when water exists).

## Age III — the eastern truth arrives

- **Fire Medicine** (火藥, "fire drug") — bombard + rocket arrows (Mysore rockets).
  "They sought eternal life. They found this."
- **The Luopan** — geomancy: settler-lens upgrade showing the land's veins; reveals a hidden
  luxury; strengthens site bonuses.
- **The Examination Hall** — imperial civil examinations: THE authority-capacity building
  family (replaces any "courthouse" naming — scholars, not bailiffs).
- **The White Gold** — porcelain: a building that MINTS a luxury no tile has (manufactured
  luxuries; the kiln lineage's crown).

## Age IV — the magister's dream of the future

- **The Paper Lantern That Lifted** — aerostats from the Kongming lantern: balloon unit,
  enormous sight, ignores terrain, cannot fight. The fog system's showpiece.
- **The Perspective Glass** — optics: sight renewals; reveals resources on explored land.
- **The Clockwork Servant** — automata (Yan Shi, al-Jazari): worker charges, or a mechanical
  worker that never expends.
- **The Loom That Remembers** — Jacquard: plantation/workshop renewals; silk becomes memory.
- **The Calculating Engine** — Babbage capstone: top science building; candidate: mints
  Magister's Dice (the machine that computes possibility feeds the reroll economy).
- **Mesmerism** — animal magnetism: convert happiness surplus into production or science
  ("the entranced workforce"); fashionably sinister.
- **The Astronomical Bureau** — the imperial star ministry: reveals the NEXT age of the star
  chart before the age opens — a tech that changes the research UI itself.
- **The Great Work** — the final tech: unlocks the **Magnum Opus** (Entry VI's golden-bead
  victory project — already named in alchemical language). The tree ends where the victory
  begins: from reading entrails to transmuting the world.

## Mechanical hooks already built that this content rides on

Renewals (improvement + building) · reveals · manufactured luxuries (new, small) ·
meter interactions (happiness/authority) · fog/sight · site bonuses/lens · dice economy ·
the Magnum Opus victory (Entry VI).
