# The Devotion deck: the full list, with the builds pass folded in

This is a review list, made on 2026-10-18 from the deck sheet (`docs/decks/devotion.md`) and the builds pass of 2026-10-17 (`docs/plans/devotion-builds.md`). It is not the spec of record. The sheet is unchanged, and its text wins where the two differ. Every card on the sheet is here, with the 36 new cards and the one tier move that the builds pass proposes.

- **Status:**
  - `implemented`: in the data, dealt, and the text is what the game does. A card the sheet calls an approximation counts here if its text was rewritten to say what was built.
  - `modify`: in the data, but not as the sheet writes it. The word after the colon says why: `revised` (the sheet changes the data row), `deferred` (not dealt, because it needs a new shape), `as built` (the game does something slightly different from the text) or `wording` (the text changes, the effect does not).
  - `add`: on the sheet, not in the data.
  - `new`: proposed by the builds pass, not on the sheet.
  - `proposed: …` after a status names a change the builds pass proposes. A moved card already sits in its proposed tier.
- **Type:** the chair a card fits: **Military**, **Economic**, **Civic** or **Wildcard**. The sheet has no Type column yet, so every type here is the builds pass's proposal ("7. The sort" for the sheet's cards, the build tables for the new ones).
- **Build:** the build or builds that the builds pass seats the card in: the faith economy, the liturgical year, the holy places, the missions, the holy war, or the shared cards of the inward builds (the liturgical year and the holy places) or the outward builds (the missions and the holy war). "Alternative" means the card is named as an alternative or under "Also fits". "Glue" means the pass calls it glue. An empty cell means no build names the card.
- **Charters and commissions** stay in their tier's table. A charter says "No chair required"; a commission starts with "Commission."
- **Doctrines:** Devotion has none yet. The builds pass says they come after the sheet is rewritten.

## Notes on the modify rows

- **Consecrated Ground:** buying a hex takes gold only. A faith price needs a second price and a second button on the city screen.
- **Mission Houses:** a proclamation presses a flat +18 more, which is 30% of the base lump, not 30% of whatever the lump has become.
- **The Settled Faith:** the proclamation half is a flat −15, which is 25% of the base lump.
- **The Doctors of the Church:** the 150% raises what follower beliefs pay a town as town lines. Their hex lines and percentages are not raised.
- **The Pious Masons:** faith pays the wonder's full purchase price, and the hammers already banked are refunded the usual way. The text reads as if faith pays only for what the wonder still needs.
- **The Interdict:** as written it needs a rite said over a town you do not own, which is a new interface. The proposed rework needs a line on a rival's Opus production, the shape Dominion's deferred Circumvallation also needs.
- **The Monastery Libraries:** the data row is deferred and still carries the old text ("Monasteries give +3 science. Libraries give +2 faith."). The Monastery is retired, so the sheet moves the science to the Temple.
- **Sacred Patronage:** deferred. The occasion the game announces is a great person spending themselves on their boon, so a card paid on the spending would pay for only some of the ways a person can be spent.
