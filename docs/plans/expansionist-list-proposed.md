# The Expansionist deck: the full list, with the themes pass folded in

This is a review list, written on 2026-10-21 and revised the same day to agree with your comments on the review page and your answers to its two questions (the themes doc, section 11). The build plan is `docs/plans/expansionist-themes-triage.md`. It puts every card on the deck sheet (`docs/decks/expansionist.md`) in one list, with every new card and change from the themes pass (`docs/plans/expansionist-themes.md`) folded in. It follows the format of the Dominion list (`docs/plans/dominion-list-proposed.md`). It is not the spec of record: the sheet is unchanged, and the sheet's text wins wherever the two differ. The sheet was read as it stands in the working tree.

- **Status:**
  - `implemented`: in the data, dealt, and the sheet's text is what the game does.
  - `modify`: in the data, but not as the sheet writes it. The word after the colon says why: `as built` (the game does something slightly different from the text) or `deferred` (not dealt, because it waits on a UX decision).
  - `add`: on the sheet, not in the data.
  - `new`: proposed by the themes pass, not on the sheet.
  - `proposed: …` after the status names a change the themes pass makes to a sheet card: a rework, a move to another tier, or a change of wording. The row already shows the change (the new text and tier), and the status says what it was.
  - `revised 2026-10-21 (section 11)` after the status: your comment on the review page changed the row. The row already shows the new text. Section 11 of the themes doc has your words, and the readings marked "to confirm".
- **Type:** the chair the card fits (Military, Economic, Civic or Wildcard), from the sheet, or from the themes pass for a new or reworked card. Charters take no chair; their type is for their face.
- **Build:** the card's theme and role in the themes pass.
  - The four themes are the raised land, the frontier, public works and the militia. Engine glue raises or spends the engine (citizens and the levy) and serves all four.
  - The roles are supply (more of the thing), widen (it pays something new), multiply (its output grows) and act (a choice or a moment). A capstone breaks a rule or doubles a category. A finisher is a late pay-off.
  - "Institution" marks a commission whose reward lasts for the rest of the game and takes no chair (`CommissionReward.lasting`).
  - "bridge" marks a card that serves a second theme.
  - "feature A" marks the card that uses the trades as a card quantity (accepted 2026-10-20).
  - "Public works" are the Granary, Aqueduct, Watermill, Harbour, Public Bath and Cistern. The word is a proposal (question 3 of the themes pass).
- **Rulings this list follows:**
  - "Direction, 2026-10-20" in `docs/decks/deck-design.md`: Expansionist is paid for growth (towns, citizens, raised land), is strongest in the middle game once its land is claimed, and gives up room; wonders leave the deck; its empire-building cards scale with towns; it does not necessarily draft slower; and your picture of it, with population as a second economic engine and public works and empire buildings as the way to grow fast.
  - Your rulings on the Scientific and Dominion passes (2026-10-21): commission rewards become lasting institutions, slow goals are lowered, a new building beats early access to an old one, and The Assay becomes a shared Expansionist and Scientific card.
  - The rulings of 2026-10-16 on the builds: improvements are the default, tended land is a splash, "Needs a levy" gates the levy readers, and roads come from caravans and foundings.
  - Your comments on this list's review page (2026-10-21, the themes doc, section 11), and your answers to its two questions: The Common Domain is cut, and of Veteran Colonies and Home for the Harvest one is cut (Home for the Harvest, the orchestrator's choice). The build uses the section's readings until you change them, and the recommendations of the themes doc's section 10 stand.
- **Doctrines:** not in this list. The sheet's 32 proposed Doctrines are yours to edit. Two of them, The Royal Works and The Builder Kings, no longer fit a theme, because they are about wonders.
- **Cut cards:** the sixteen sheet cards the themes pass and your comments cut are in the last section, `## Cut by this pass`, with their sheet text and one line on why, so you can restore any of them. Home for the Harvest, a new card, is noted there too (revised 2026-10-21, section 11).
