# Batches Z1, Z2 and Z3: the tidy-up after the Devotion and Magisterial builds

**Status:** the spec, written 2026-10-26 before any agent starts. Every Devotion and Magisterial card is built (plans `docs/plans/devotion-themes-triage.md` and `docs/plans/magisterial-themes-triage.md`, with "Built" notes). The batch agents reported small faults they found but left outside their fences. This file lists the ones to fix before the push to `main` and the playtest. The user's direction, 2026-10-25: *"lets continue with the build. Once everything is implemented, playtesting can help us determine what's good and isn't"*.

Two batches, with disjoint files, so they can run at the same time:

- **Z2** fixes words: card text, describer words, comments and docs.

## Z1: rules and code

1. **The worker's build command takes the wrong builders** (found in MG9, older). `improvementError` accepts a `buildImprovement` command from a prophet (a Holy Site) and from a great person (its own work); both unit rows carry `charges`. A Holy Site laid this way skips `plantHolySiteAt` (no religion founded, no belief drawn), and a work laid this way skips `spendGreatPerson` (no legacy, no tally, no spent moment). Fix: the build command accepts only the units whose job is to build improvements. A prophet plants through `plantHolySite` and a great person lays a work through its own verb. Pin it with a test that the command is refused for both, with the state unchanged.
2. **Owned land its owner cannot see** (found in MG15, older). A hex owned more than `cities.claimRadius` (3) from its town is not lit by its owner's sight of its own ground (`visibility.ts`), and `ownedTiles` does not list it. Citadels, outposts, The Landmark Bounds and The Paradise Garden all make such hexes. Fix: an empire always sees every hex it owns. Which hexes a town may work stays as it is. Test it with a citadel's claim.
3. **A bought wonder raises no wonder notice** (found in MG11, older). `applyPurchaseItem` does not put a claimed wonder on `CommandResult.wonders`, so the buyer gets no notice and rivals are not told of their refunds. A completion and a chop already do this. Fix it the same way, for gold, faith and culture.
4. **Two claims keep the loser's pin** (found in MG16). The citadel's `claimAround` (`greatPeople.ts`) and The Encroaching Fields' `runEncroachment` (`frontiers.ts`) write `state.tileOwner` directly and keep the losing town's pin on the hex. Move both onto `seizeTile` (`cities.ts`), the one writer that moves a hex from a rival's town, so the pin is dropped and the towns and sight are refreshed in one way. Keep every existing behaviour the tests pin.
5. **The Mission can count one town twice in a turn** (found in DV6). Under a Pentecost chain, a town can turn, turn away and turn again in one turn; the `foreignCityConverted` tally counts each turning. Fix: the tally counts a town at most once a turn for each religion, read off the same stamp as the moment (`City.convertedOn`). This changes reading 39 of the Devotion build; record that in the test's comment.
6. **The free reroll names the wrong card** (found in MG2). `explainRerollCost` (`religion.ts`) always labels the free reroll's price line "The Rihla", even when the rule comes from The Second Reading. Fix: name the row that holds the `rerollOffers` rule. `test/sim/leaders.test.ts` pins the old label.
7. **Two offers at once** (found in MG7). During an adoption, a rider's culture windfall can open an Order hand before the Doctrine draw, so both offers wait at once. Check that the game and the screens handle that state: both can be answered, in either order, and neither hides the other. Fix only what is broken, and add a test.

## Z3: a charter that opens a building or a unit prints what it opens

The user, 2026-10-28: *"ok lets fix that bug for now"*.

**The fault.** `describeCard` (`src/sim/statecraft/describers.ts`) prints a charter's grant only when the charter opens a technology (`charterTechClauses`), a work (`unlocks.improvement`) or rites (`unlocks.rites`). It prints nothing for `unlocks.building` or `unlocks.unit`. Three dealt cards have such a charter and no effects, so their face in the Order hand is blank: The Planters' Charter (the Plantation House), Numidian Cavalry (the Numidian Cavalry unit) and The Chartered Company (the Company Guard). The collection, the council and every other surface that prints `describeCard` show the same blank.

**The fix.**
1. In `describeCard`, beside the work and the rites clauses, print one clause for `charter.unlocks.building` and one for `charter.unlocks.unit`. Word them like the clauses a technology charter already prints for the same things (`charterTechClauses`), led by "once drafted", and end with "this card takes no chair", as the work clause does.
   - **A building:** name it with a keyword mark, then what it does in its own words (`describeBuildingRow`, through `liveWords`). A `purchaseOnly` row says it is bought, and in which bank, not built.
   - **A unit:** name it with a keyword mark. A unit that only a purchase brings names its bank, and the building its purchase needs if the row names one (the Company Guard: gold, in a town with a Barracks). Then the unit's own effects in the vocabulary's words (the Numidian Cavalry's strength for each kind of luxury), if the game already has a describer for a unit row's effects; do not write a second one.
   - If the game holds the unit or building back until a technology or an age, say so, the way the early-unit clause names its gate. If you cannot find such a gate, say nothing about one.
2. A register test: every dealt Order (`orderIsDealt`) prints at least one live clause in `describeCard`. Every dealt row passes it after the fix.
3. Pins for the three cards' printed words.

**Not in the fence:** the card rows' `text` (the sheets' words stay, and the sheets keep "modify: as built" for The Planters' Charter and Numidian Cavalry); the Compendium's own charter line (`charterClause`), which already names what a charter opens. No schema change: nothing in the state changes.
