E2, final

The user picked E2 (one drawer, two depths) and asked for two changes: commissions on the left side outside the empire menu, and research and culture as clickable sections instead of separate Tech tree and Statecraft buttons. This page draws the result. The written spec, with every state, size, key and the code each part replaces, is final-e2.md beside it.

What changed from E2

Commissions are see-through chips in the left column under the empire panel, outside the drawer, as in the approved mockup. The drawer opens to the right of the panel, so the chips are never covered and never move. Research and Culture are each one large button: clicking Research opens the star chart, clicking Culture opens Statecraft; they show a pointer, a coloured left edge, an underlined title and a moving arrow on hover, and a coloured ring on keyboard focus. The at-rest bars are not separate targets: the whole panel is one.

What the user said

  • “Let’s have commissions appear on the left side outside of the empire menu.”
  • “Lets have the research and culture sections be clickable (and reflected in the UX as such) instead of dedicated civic/tech tree buttons.”

Where the tech tree is

  • The labelled Tech tree button at the top of the drawer: hover, then one click.
  • On touch: two taps. T as before.

Touch and keyboard

  • The first tap opens depth two directly.
  • Tab focus opens depth one; Q toggles depth two.

Risks

  • The tech tree is never one click from rest: the audit’s hidden-door problem, softened by hover.
  • No research name at rest.
  • Crossing the corner can open it; needs the 200 ms delay.

At rest

  • Empire panel 200 × 56: banner (red dot when owed), research and draft bars with turns, ▸.
  • One target: hover opens depth one; click, tap, Enter or Q pins depth two.
  • Commission chips 200 × 38 under it, only for seated cards; at most four, then “+N more”.

Drawer

  • Opens to the right of the panel (left 220, top 54, 300 wide), so it never covers the chips and nothing moves.
  • Depth one: the Research and Culture sections, each one big button.
  • Depth two: adds Religion (H), Leader (L), Age wager, Supply.

Why the bars are not targets

  • 6 px tall: far below a usable target (WCAG 2.2 asks for 24 px).
  • Splitting the panel was E1’s design, not picked.
  • The sections are one hover away; T and C go direct.

Footprint at rest

  • 22,975 px² (1.9 %) with no commission seated.
  • ≈38,175 px² (3.1 %) with two seated.
  • Today: 70,101 px² (5.7 %).
