How the board shows the citizens' hexes

Your comment on the city screen: "could we do a design pass on the hex assignments? it's a bit unclear right now". These are diagrams on a plain hex map, not the game's board. The town is in the middle. Eight citizens work the land, and you pinned two of them. The five hexes at the left belong to the next town, Pisac. The outer ring and the mountains cannot be worked.

Today

A small bone ring means a citizen works the hex. A ring in your colour means you pinned it. Nothing shows which hexes are free to take, which belong to the next town, or what a hex pays.

  • Hard to see on light ground.
  • A ring does not say "a person".
  • No difference between "free" and "not yours".

A. Citizens as pieces

Each worked hex has a citizen on it, with the yields under them. A free hex has an empty dashed seat and faint yields. A pinned citizen is in your colour with a lock. A hex the next town works carries that town's name.

  • Reads as "a person stands here".
  • The board stays bright, so the marks compete with the terrain.
  • Click an empty seat to send a citizen; click a citizen to pin or free them.

B. Light and shade

Worked hexes stay bright with a bone outline. Every other hex goes dark: free hexes a little, hexes you cannot use a lot. Yields show on every hex you can use. A pinned hex has a lock.

  • The town's working land reads at one look, even zoomed out.
  • No figure of a citizen, so "worked" is only brightness.
  • It darkens most of the screen while the city is open.

C. Both (recommended)

Worked hexes are bright, outlined, and carry a citizen and the yields. Free hexes are slightly shaded with their yields. Hexes you cannot use are dark, and the next town's hexes carry its name. A pinned hex has your colour on its outline and a lock.

  • Three clear states: worked, free, not available.
  • The citizen and the lock say who decided: the town or you.
  • The legend below sits at the bottom centre of the city screen, beside Buy tiles.
Legend on the screen: worked pinned by you free to work not available
