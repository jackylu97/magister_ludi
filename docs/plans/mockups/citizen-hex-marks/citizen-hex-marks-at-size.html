B and C at the size of the game

A mock-up of the city screen at 1440 by 900, with twelve citizens on the land. The terrain is a drawing with fields, mines, trees and hills on it, so the marks have something to compete with. The panels are plain boxes at their new widths. This is not the game's board.

B. Light and shade, with an outline

  • A worked hex is bright with a bone outline. No other mark.
  • A hex you pinned has the outline in your colour and a small lock.
  • A free hex is lightly shaded. A hex that is not yours is dark.

C. The same, with one dot for the citizen

  • Everything in B, plus one plain dot on each worked hex.
  • The dot is bone for a citizen the town placed, and your colour for one you pinned. So the dot replaces the lock.
