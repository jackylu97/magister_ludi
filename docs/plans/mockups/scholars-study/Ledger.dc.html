The book

The sheet opens as a book with two pages. The red money rules and the double rule come from an account book.

Each figure sits in a tabular column. A nil is a faint dash. The bars and the sparklines go.

Left page: this turn

The rows are the eight sources, in the order of the game. The columns are the six yields.

Upkeep is a debit: red ink and a true minus sign, in the gold column. Each source keeps its ink in a small square, so the colours stay easy to see.

Right page: one yield

The chip that opened the sheet chooses the yield. Here it is production. The tabs at the edge open the other yields.

The last eight turns replace the curve. Each row is one turn, with the three sources that you chose: statecraft, great people and religion.

Your cards

Your cards is the part of this turn that the deck made, in the ink of statecraft. Confirm counts up the same figure on the Statecraft sheet.
