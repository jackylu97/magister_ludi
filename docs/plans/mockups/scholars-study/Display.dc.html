The display face

Only the display face changes. Fraunces, Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono stay. Here are six candidates in place of Instrument Serif. Each one sets the same game copy at its real size.

All six come from Google Fonts. Candidate F has no italic, so its emphasis is the red ink alone.

The board title and these notes use the faces of today.

a · more historicf · more modern

ASorts Mill Goudy

The most historic. An old-style book face from 1915: warm and bookish, with a soft and lively italic.

Risk. One weight only, and thin strokes. The italic can be faint at 15px on parchment.

Risk. The roman is the playful part. The italic is calmer than the italic of today.
