After: the age plate

approved

The plate shows the numeral, the deeds drawn with the deck emblems, and one plain line. It needs no picture. Later, a gouache plate can sit behind it, as row four of the splash-art plan says.

Lapis and gilt come from the painted frontispiece. Only the big moments use them: the turn card, the age plate and the Opus.

Motion: the plate settles on cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). The numeral is pressed in like a seal. Then the deeds come up from left to right. Reduced motion keeps a fade and the same order.

The plate is also the first page of the age in the Annals (art pass L4). The deeds and figures here are a sample.

The motion

About six seconds in all. A key at any time goes straight to the standings.

Reduced motion: no camera move and no turn. The bead slide stays, because the slide is the event.

The page uses the 3D Abacus as it is: its frame, its slide, its clack, its sway and its turntable. Two things are new: the camera move and the golden bead on the rod.

Today the frame leaves the golden bead off. Only the register under the frame shows it, in its golden slot. The register fills that slot at step 5.
