After: the age plate

The numeral, the deeds drawn with the mark families (the deck emblems, the tech marks), and one plain line. It is finished with no painting; a gouache plate can sit behind it later, as splash-art row four plans.

Lapis and gilt come from the frontispiece painting and are used only on announcement surfaces: the turn card, the age plate, the Opus.

Motion: the plate settles on cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), the numeral is pressed in like a seal, and the deeds come up left to right. Reduced motion keeps a fade and the same reading order.

It is also the age’s frontispiece in the Annals (art pass L4). Deeds and figures here are a sample.

The last page

The loss line names the winner and the work. It used to read: “The reckoning has gone elsewhere.”

The winner reads: “You finished The Magnum Opus in Baghdad. The game is yours.”

The table stays a real table, ruled like an account page: hairlines between rows, figures in tabular columns, and the standing under the accountant’s double rule. Columns are ordered by total, so the leader is the first column.
