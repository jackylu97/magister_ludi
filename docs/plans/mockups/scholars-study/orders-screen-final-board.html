The orders screen: the final board

This is the board of record for the screen. It puts the approved face 1, the emblem, into round four's structure: the head, the gilt register, the chairs, the hand. It settles the arithmetic that the taller card left open, and it ends with the build spec.

Card names and texts are live rows. The live figures are samples. A dagger marks the one new reading. Nothing here is built.

The answer in short

  • Nothing scrolls at 1440 by 900, 1280 by 720 or 1024 by 768. All 11 chairs are in view at each size.
  • At 1440 by 900 the face is the approved one, 210 by 258, in two rows of six.
  • On smaller windows the face takes a compact form. It is the same emblem with the same parts in the same order; only the picture band is shorter. At 1024 wide the card is also narrower.
  • The register is 104 px tall at every size. The government and each Doctrine are in the Doctrine frame, laid wide: the device at the left, the name and the effect at the right.
  • The cost is the hand. With 7 chairs or more the hand is a rail of card tops and shows no names. H opens it over the table.

  • A hand card is face 1's "in the hand" state: 104 by 162, the tier plate, the device, the name. It has no effect text.
  • 12 cards or fewer, and room: the hand cards lie side by side, 130 by 202 while the table is one row (see the 5-chair screen).
  • Otherwise a rail: one overlapped rank, cut to 28 px, sorted by type with 10 px between types. It shows card tops only. Hover or focus on the rail lifts the card under the pointer and shows its full face (moment 1).
  • H opens the whole hand over the table: two ranks of 15, 85 px apart, every name in view. A click picks a card up and closes the view, so the chairs can be seen.
  • D opens the binder: every Order you hold as a full face, six to a row, with filters by type and deck and a search box. A seated card carries its chair's number. It scrolls.
  • Both views are layers inside the sheet. Esc closes the layer first, and then the sheet.

  • There is no foot bar. The keys are: H the hand, D the binder, arrows to move, Enter to pick up or put down, 1 to 9 to seat in that chair, Esc to put a card back, close a layer, or close the sheet.
  • A refusal (a sealed chair, a card that does not fit) prints the game's own sentence in place of the hint for 4 seconds.

The border, by rarity (drawn elements, outside in)

  • Common: outer border; a plain paper band; inner rule.
  • Uncommon: outer border; a band hatched at 45 degrees (ink at 34%, 1 px lines 3.2 px apart); inner rule; four corner flourishes, 13 px, inside the inner rule.
  • Rare: outer border; a gilt band (a 135 degree gradient of the three gilts) with a punched pattern (deep-gilt dots 1.4 px wide on a 4.2 px grid); inner rule; four eight-pointed stars in the corners; the tier plate in ink with pale gilt letters; the picture's ground in punched gilt, with a sun and a moon in its top corners.
  • Doctrine: as rare, with a 7 px band, a deep-gilt outer border, a deep-gilt plate that says "Doctrine" (or "Tier 31" on the government), and a sun, a moon and two stars in the corners.

The device (all measures as a share of the device's width)

  • Rays: 32 lines round the centre, from 37.5% out to 48% and 44% in turn, 1% wide, round ends, in the type's ink. Left out when the card pays nothing now.
  • Disc: radius 34%, paper, hatched at 45 degrees in the type's ink (lines 0.9% wide, 3.2% apart, at 34% strength).
  • Rings: radius 34% at 1.4% wide, and radius 31% at 0.7% wide, in the type's ink.
  • Suit: the deck's suit from suitFace.ts, 50% of the device's width, centred, in the type's ink. A card of no deck shows the lozenge seal.
  • Cut shadow: the same suit in ink at 30%, moved 1.5% right and down, under the suit.
  • Size: the device is 49.5% of the card's width in the full form. It may overlap the picture band's edges; the band clips it.

The rules for the words

  • The figure tablet shows the first figure of the card's live reading, not of its sentence: the first yield of the per-turn stamp ("+15" with the gold mark), a Scaler's first value, or a commission's count ("3/4"). A card with no number (a rule card, a staged card) has no tablet. A card that pays nothing now shows "+0" in grey.
  • The red initial is the first letter of the effect. A text that starts with a figure or a sign ("+3% production...") gets no initial, and its figure is red as usual. The register's wide form has no initial.
  • The sentence is the describers' clause, whole. Figures and yield words are red and semibold; a named thing is in small capitals.
  • The colophon is the live line: "pays +15 this turn", "its value now +4 +4", beads and "3 of 4 towns reached", "in force", "+0 now", or "awaits your seal: on Confirm".

States

  • Sealed: a 5 px cord of two red strands across the card at the foot of the picture, a wax seal 28 px wide on it with the turns left, and a lock with the turns in the chair tab. The card cannot be lifted.
  • Unmet: no rays, "+0" in the tablet, "+0 now" in the colophon, and under the effect an italic reason line (full and large forms only).
  • Staged: a dashed outer border, no hard shadow, the device and the effect at 55%, no tablet, the colophon "awaits your seal: on Confirm", and a gilt tag "played, not confirmed" on the top border.
  • Empty chair: a dashed outline of the card's size in the chair type's lit ink, the chair's mark at 50%, three lines ("An empty chair. It takes Economic cards. It pays nothing."), and the chair tab.
  • Fits the card in hand: a 2 px ring in --y-gold-lit, 3 px off the card, and a tag "swap" or "seat here". Chairs that do not fit are at 40%.

Breakpoints

  • Window 1400 by 860 or more: the full form (the large form with 6 chairs or fewer).
  • Under 860 tall, 1240 wide or more: the compact form, 6 columns.
  • Under 1240 wide: the compact narrow form, 6 columns. Under about 1000 wide the table may scroll sideways.
  • The hand is side by side with 12 cards or fewer when the table is one row; otherwise the rail.

Readings

  • Existing: the per-turn figure and the occasion form (explainCardImpact, the card stamp), a Scaler's value (scalerFigure.ts), a commission's counter (commissionCounters), the staged mark (pendCardStamp), a seal's turns (sealRemaining), a Doctrine's stamp, the head's total (foldDeck), the fit and the refusals (chairFitFor, placeError, removeError).

Left for later

Where the taller face forced a compromise

The hand. With 7 chairs or more it is a rail of card tops with no names, at every window size. H is needed to read the hand.

Small windows. The approved 210 by 258 face is used only at 1400 by 860 and up. Below that the compact form has a 44 px picture band, so the device is smaller and partly clipped, and one text in ten is cut at 1280 by 720.

The register. It is 104 px, so a Doctrine's effect has four lines and the longest are cut until lifted.
