The orders screen, round four

This is the board to build from. It is Way A of round three: large played cards that carry their own effect. The effect is now set as part of the card. The Doctrines have a place of their own at the head of the table.

The page has four parts: the played card, the Doctrines, the whole screen at four window sizes, and a short spec for the build. A choice is drawn side by side only where one remains. Card names and texts are live rows. The figures on the live lines are samples. Nothing here is built.

1The played card

Three treatments of the same three cards, at the size they have with 11 chairs (210 by 194), and at 2x.

  • Chosen: treatment 2, the suit in the paper. In the hand a card shows its picture: the suit, the tier, the name. When it is played, the suit sinks into the paper and the words come forward. So the two faces are one card in two states, and the change is the moment of play.
  • Why. It gives the effect the most room and the largest type (12.5 px against 12), set left with a steady measure, which is what five lines of rules text need. The suit stays large, so the card still reads as a card and not as a text box.
  • From Way C: the saying is always readable. The card is wider than a tarot card (210 by 194, not 108 by 168) so that the whole effect fits. Every text in the sample fits at 1440, 1280 and 1024.
  • The order inside the card. A strip in the chair's ink: the chair's mark, number and type, a lock while sealed, the tier at the right. The name in Fraunces 15 px. The effect in Instrument Sans 12.5 px, line height 1.36, about 32 letters to a line. A foot band in the type's pale ground with the live line in 12 px mono.
  • The words. Figures are tabular mono. Yield words take their yield's ink. Named things (a Granary) are bold, as the game's keywords are. The drawing styles the data text; the build prints the describers' clauses, which already carry the marks.
  • Type, rarity, state. The frame and the suit take the type's ink. The inner rule is the rarity: one faint rule, a pewter double rule, or a black double rule with a black tier plate. A sealed card has wax on its edge with the turns left.

The states of the chosen card.

2The Doctrines

A Doctrine is offered each time you adopt a government. You may pass it. One you take is kept for the whole game and is never seated. A wonder or a commission can grant another offer.

  • How many. The ladder has four adoptions (tiers 4, 10, 18 and 26), so four is the usual full set, and grants can add more. I found no cap in the code I read. The bot games hold 3.9 at turn 200. The register is drawn for four and takes five at 1440; with six the cards narrow and the effect is cut at four lines.
  • Chosen: (a) the gilt register. The government and its Doctrines are one row of wide gilt cards at the head of the table, above the chairs. Each Doctrine shows its name, its whole effect at 12 px and a live line from the same reading the Orders use. The government card carries its law and the next draft's progress.
  • Why. Doctrines are permanent law, so they belong with the government and above the Orders, and they must be read without the pointer. The fan in (b) is prettier and fails that.

3The whole screen

The head, then the gilt register, then the chairs, then the hand. One played card was laid this visit (chair 6), one pays nothing now (chair 5), three are sealed, and chair 11 is empty.

Eleven chairs, 30 cards in the hand, four Doctrines. 1440 by 900, true size.

  • Columns. A played card is never narrower than 184 px. The table takes as many columns as fit, at most 6, and as many rows as the chairs need. Up to 6 chairs is always one row.
  • Card height. The rows share the height left after the register and the hand, between 130 and 240 px.
  • Effect size. 14 px on a one-row table, 12.5 px when the card is 190 px tall or more, 12 px from 160, 11.5 px under that. Never under 11.
  • Register. 140 px with its caption line when the window is 860 px tall or more. Otherwise 92 to 100 px with no caption and the effect on three lines. At 1280, one Doctrine of four is cut; at 1024, four of five register texts are cut and open on hover.
  • The hand. Full faces while it holds 12 cards or fewer and there is room. Then one rank of indexed cards. On a short window, a rail of tops. H always opens the whole hand over the table as round two's two ranks, and D opens the binder.
  • Played texts cut: none of the 10 at any of the three sizes, in this sample.

The moments

4For the build

Sizes and spacing

  • Step: 4 px. Sheet 24 px from the window sides, 16 px under the bar and above the foot. Sheet padding 24 (16 on a compact window). Cloth padding 16 (12 compact).
  • Head: 52 px (44 compact): title, hint, what your cards pay, Revert, Confirm, close.
  • Register: government card 280 px wide (240, 190 compact), Doctrine cards share the rest, 8 px apart. 12 px to the table.
  • Table: 10 px between cards, 12 px between rows (8 compact).
  • Played card: frame 2.5 px, radius 8, inner rule at 3 px. Strip 26 px. Body padding 8 by 12. Foot band 29 px. Hard shadow 4 by 4 px.
  • Hand rank: cards 104 by 162, about 40 px apart, 10 px more between types. Index edge 38 px wide.

Type steps

  • 28 px Newsreader, the sheet title. 19 px Fraunces, the government. 15 px Fraunces, a card or Doctrine name (17 on a one-row table, 14 compact).
  • 12.5 px Instrument Sans, the effect (14, 12 or 11.5 by the rule above). 12 px, a Doctrine's effect.
  • 12 px IBM Plex Mono, tabular, the live line and every figure. 11 px mono, the strip and the small labels. Nothing under 11.

Colours, as the game's tokens

  • Cloth --lampblack, with --gilt at about 55% for its edge rule. Paper --parchment. Text --ink, --ink-soft.
  • Card frame, suit and strip rule: --type-military, --type-economic, --type-civic, --type-wildcard. Strip and foot grounds: the matching --type-*-ground. The suit in the paper is the type ink at 11%.
  • Rarity: --ink-line, --rank-pewter, --ink.
  • Doctrine and government cards: --gilt frames, --deep-gilt for small gilt text on paper.
  • The fit ring and the tags: --y-gold-lit. Wax: --sealing-wax. Confirm: --cinnabar. Yield words and figures: the six --y-* inks on paper.

Breakpoints

  • Window height under 860 px: the compact register and the hand rail.
  • Columns follow the 184 px rule, so 6 from about 1240 px wide, 5 from about 1060, 4 at 1024.

Readings

  • Existing: the per-turn figure and the occasion form (explainCardImpact, the card stamp), a Scaler's value (scalerFigure.ts), a commission's counter (commissionCounters), "on Confirm" for a staged card (pendCardStamp), a seal's turns (sealRemaining), a Doctrine's stamp, the head's total (foldDeck).
  • New: the reason line beside "+0 now".
  • The head's total stays the Ledger's figure. The board does not present it as the sum of the cards' lines.
  • "In force" is a word, not a reading: a rule card that is seated and confirmed.

For a later pass

  • The card back, real drag, the draft restyled in this language, and the live art for the chair marks.

Choices that remain

The hand with 11 chairs: one rank with names set down the card's edge (drawn), or a tighter fan with names only under the pointer.

The card treatment: 2 is used. Say if 1 or 3 reads better to you.

The wax seal: on the card's right edge (drawn), or over the foot band.

More than five Doctrines: narrower cards with cut text (drawn rule), or a second register row.

Carried over: the cloth (lampblack is drawn), and what a seated "On draft" card shows.
