The seats. Each seat shows the relation, the turn a war began or a truce ends, and each running deal with its turns left. The tabs are gone: nothing about a rival is on a second page.

The luxury rule. Greyed: the other side has the luxury now, but only by a deal that will end. The row gives the source and the turns left. Not shown: both sides have their own.

What they will accept. Before you send, the sheet knows three things: what the rules refuse, a last copy (the rival keeps it), and a luxury that the rival already has. The two questions ask the rival for the rest.

Made to grow. Each kind of item is one group, and each row has four parts: mark, name, amount or term, and what it costs or gives. A new kind is a new group. A new action is one more button in its row.

How it waits. The notice does not block End Turn, today or here. An offer stays until you answer it, the rival takes it back, or a war starts. The chip beside End Turn counts the offers and opens the card.

The answers. Accept and Refuse are in the rules today. The rules have no counter-offer. “Change it at the table” opens the sheet with these terms on the table, and you send your own deal from there. For an offer of peace, the buttons are “Accept peace” and “Send the envoy home”.

  1. 1Colour. Today the sheet has its own olive greens and grey paper, and a green primary button. After: parchment, iron-gall ink and one cinnabar button.
  2. 2Labels and size. Today a small letterspaced label sits over each heading, and the hints are 9 to 11 px in a faint grey. After: no label over a heading, and no text below 12 px.
  3. 3Elements. Today: shield seals, two tabs, a quote with a side stripe, tick cards and a third column for the paper. After: the approved banner and war seal, no tabs, one row for each item, and one bar for the deal and its buttons.
  4. 4The notice. Today: a button with a hard shadow that says “1 offer waiting”. It shows no terms and it opens the sheet. After: a flat card in the family of the news toasts, with the terms and the answers on it.
