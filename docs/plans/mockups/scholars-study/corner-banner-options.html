The second round is first: three takes on the corner seal cut as a short, wide pennant. The first round, with four variants, is below it. Each card is drawn at the game's size, 248 px wide, under a piece of the top bar. The charges and the seat colours are the game's own.

Second round: the seal as a pennant

Your answer to the first round: Variant B, with the Q mark centred, and joined with the pennant, shorter and wider. These are three close takes on that. In each one the pennant, the Q key and the arrow share one centre line, and the whole column from the card's top to the row of four marks is the grow handle.

Costs

Pick

Build Take 1, the swallowtail pennant on the corner. It has the largest charge (28 px), the Q key and the arrow are on the pennant's centre line, and the card, the 48 px text edge and the open card do not change.

If the pennant should look more like a button, add the ink shadow of Take 3 to the swallowtail. That is a change of one rule.

Why it looks bad

  • The charge is the smallest mark on the card. It is 10 px wide. The four marks in the row below are 16 px. At 10 px the three charges above cannot be told apart.
  • The column is mostly empty. It is 40 × 92 px, 16% of the card's width. The banner covers 16 × 26 px of it, about 11%. The arrow covers 8 × 6 px. About 50 px of blank paper lies between them.
  • It loses to everything beside it. The technology name is 13 px semibold, and the two bars are 184 px of saturated lapis and grape. A light seat colour, such as papyrus, almost disappears on the parchment.
  • The handle does not look like a handle. The click area is 40 × 92 px = 3,680 px², but only 464 px² of it is drawn (416 px² of banner, 48 px² of arrow). The arrow is far from the banner, so the two do not read as one control.
  • The red dot is as large as the charge. The dot is 9 px and the charge is 10 px. When a decision waits, the dot covers the corner of the canton.

Recommendation

Build Variant B, the corner seal. The charge goes from 10 px to 26 px and the handle looks like a thing to press, and the card, the text edge and the open card do not change.

If you want the charge larger still and can accept narrower lines, take Variant A. All four draw a thin ink edge and use the second colour as a frame, so a light seat colour still shows on the parchment.
