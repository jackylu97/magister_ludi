Contrast and colour roles

Parchment, iron-gall ink and gilt stay, as the art direction settled. What changes is that every pigment has one job, and every pairing that carries text is worked out and printed here. Text holds 4.5:1 or better on every ground it sits on.

Before: one red for the button, the refusal, the shortage and the label, so it meant none of them.

The primary action

Cinnabar fills the one primary control on a screen, such as Confirm. Only End Turn and the turn card add the hard shadow, because they are played.

A refusal

Borders frozen: authority is overdrawn.

A drawn manicule in red leads the line. The reducer’s sentence stays in ink, said once beside the thing refused.

A shortage

Gold a turn−4.6−3

A debit is red ink, as in an account book: always with a true minus sign, only in the figure column. On the dark top bar it uses a lighter red.
