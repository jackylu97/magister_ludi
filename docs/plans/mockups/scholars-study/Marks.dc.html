Icons and glyphs

Every mark is drawn in the house hand: one stroke weight, round caps and joins, the game’s own objects. No font characters stand in for icons, and nothing arrives in a platform’s colours. Marks read at 12 to 16 px sit on Tabler’s 24-unit grid at a 2.75 stroke; tech marks sit on the house 64 grid at 6.5.

The fallback rule

A tech nobody has drawn yet shows its age numeral pressed in a seal. Never an emoji. A source test keeps every glyph string from reaching the page except through the printer.
