Motion, in captions

Sheets settle

From 8 px below, in 240 ms, on cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). The curve today overshoots by 0.12 px, and nobody can see that. So for the sheets, this is a choice of curve, not a visible change.

The great-person card is the one visible bounce today: about 9 px and 2% in scale, over 500 ms. It settles instead. The pop of the turn card to 1.011× goes too.

Only the stamp overshoots

The wax seal on Confirm keeps its overshoot, because it is a physical event. The numeral on the age plate uses the same press.
