What changed in this revision

Rites: fourteen towns on one screen (seven illustrative), in narrower rows: free towns first, then towns keeping a rite, soonest free first. Click a rite in the strip and the free towns re-order by what it would add in each, best first, each with a one-click "Start · 72" button. Once the bank cannot pay for another, the remaining buttons grey out with "short N". Click the rite again or press Escape to leave. With no rite selected, each free town keeps its "Start a rite" menu. "Start in several…" is gone: the rite mode does that job.

Bonuses: unchanged.

Religion & followers: the god cards, the follower belief cards and every bonus figure are removed. The wheel, the religion's name and facts, who follows, rival pressure and the clergy remain, rearranged around a larger wheel.

Order of the rows

  • Free towns first, in founding order; then towns keeping a rite, soonest free first.
  • With a rite selected, the free towns re-order by what it would add there, best first, and slide into place (instant with reduced motion).
  • A town started this turn joins the kept group at its head, marked "started now".

Settled by the user

  • One click starts a rite, and the button carries the price ("Start · 72"). No confirm dialog. Once the bank cannot pay for another, every remaining button greys out and says "short N".
  • A rite that pays two things ranks by its main yield, the second shown beside it (Consecration of the Bounds: culture, then "borders +30%").
  • Selecting the same rite again, or Escape, leaves the mode.

What else follows

  • "Start in several…" is gone: select a rite and click down the list. Each click is one performRite, so no batch command is needed.
  • Blessing of Arms adds the same +10 defence everywhere, so its ranking is a tie and the banner says so; the rows keep founding order.
  • A rite you lack is greyed in the strip with its card and cannot be selected.

The opening, beat by beat

  • 0 ms: the faith bar's slices grow left to right, 90 ms apart; the headline counts up to +87 and lands.
  • Cards: each is dealt face down and turns over in 420 ms, 110 ms after the one before it: the Orders draft's deal (card-deal, DEAL_STAGGER_MS).
  • Figures: as a card lands, its stamp holds for 220 ms, counts up over 750 ms, then lands with the overshoot and the glow: playCardStamp's four beats.
  • With reduced motion on, nothing moves: the cards are face up and every figure is simply there.

Hover

  • A card lifts and casts the Orders hand's shadow (.sc-card-order:hover).
  • Its slice of the faith bar lights and the rest dim, so "where does my faith come from" is answered by pointing.
  • The card's hover card (as the Order's) lists the Ledger lines behind its figure, town by town.

When it plays

  • The Orders screen counts only on Confirm and lands stamps at rest, and the Religion sheet's rule is "the stamp lands here, it never plays". The user's request changes that for this tab.
  • Proposed: the deal plays every time the tab opens (it is short); a figure counts only if it changed since you last looked, otherwise it lands. Needs a ruling.

Settled, and still open

Settled by the user

F1, three tabs. The Rites tab is built on your towns. The Bonuses tab is cards and animation. The Religion & followers tab carries no god or belief cards. Free towns first. One click starts a rite, the button carries the price, and the rest grey out with "short N" once the bank cannot pay; no confirm dialog. A two-yield rite ranks by its main yield, the second beside it. Selecting the rite again, or Escape, leaves the mode.

  1. When the Bonuses tab animates.

    The Religion sheet's rule today is "the stamp lands here, it never plays", and the Orders screen counts only on Confirm. Proposed: the deal plays each time the tab opens; a figure counts up only when it changed since you last looked, otherwise it lands.

  2. Which figure the god and belief cards wear on the Bonuses tab.

    The Ledger's attribution (proposed; sums to the Ledger; Star Readers +8) or today's ghost stamp (what removing it would cost; +10).

  3. Which seated cards appear.

    Devotion cards and any other card that pays faith (proposed; The Camp Chaplains, Dominion, is +49 of +87), or Devotion only.

  4. The prophet's rite on the Rites tab.

    Offer it here (proposed), or only on the prophet's own sheet.

  5. Rival pressure rows.

    Towns you can see now only, or remembered towns too under the faith lens's fog rule.

  6. The order of towns keeping a rite.

    Soonest free first (drawn), or founding order.

  7. From the first round.

    Whether the drawer's Religion dot also lights when a town is free and a rite is affordable; whether the sheet is called "Faith" or "Religion".
