# The Scientific deck: the full list, with the themes pass folded in

This is a review list, rebuilt on 2026-10-20 and updated twice on 2026-10-21, the second time for your review comments (`docs/plans/scientific-themes.md` section 14). It puts every card on the deck sheet (`docs/decks/scientific.md`) in one list, with every new card and change from the themes pass (`docs/plans/scientific-themes.md`) folded in. It replaces the list of 2026-10-18, which folded in the superseded builds pass of 2026-10-17. It is not the spec of record: the sheet is unchanged, and the sheet's text wins wherever the two differ. You asked to read it grouped by tier, as before, and to cut cards by hand (2026-10-20: *"68 chairs is fine, i can cut on my own once they're formatted."*).

- **Status:**
  - `implemented`: in the data, dealt, and the sheet's text is what the game does.
  - `modify`: in the data, but not as the sheet writes it. The word after the colon says why: `revised` (the sheet changes the card) or `deferred` (not dealt, because it needs a new shape). Some rows have both.
  - `add`: on the sheet, not in the data.
  - `new`: proposed by the themes pass, not on the sheet. Some came from the 2026-10-17 pass, which the themes pass marks "(10-17)".
  - `proposed: …` after the status names a change the themes pass makes to a sheet card: a tier move, a rarity change, a rework, a trim to one effect, or a change of wording. The row already shows the change (the new tier, rarity and text), and the status says what it was.
  - **(was …)** names the data row that a renamed card takes over.
- **Type:** the chair the card fits (Military, Economic, Civic or Wildcard), from the themes pass's type sort. Charters take no chair; their type is for their face.
- **Build:** the card's theme and role in the themes pass.
  - The four themes are the learned land, institutions, great cities and the elite army. Engine glue raises or spends the engine (technologies and charters) and serves all four.
  - The roles are supply (more of the thing), widen (it pays something new), multiply (its output grows) and act (a choice or a moment). A capstone breaks a rule or doubles a category.
  - A second theme after a semicolon marks a bridge between two themes.
  - "feature A" or "feature B" marks a card that uses one of the two features you accepted on 2026-10-20: the trades as a card quantity, and reading rivals.
- **Rulings this list follows** (2026-10-20, `docs/plans/scientific-themes.md` section 11):
  - Tier 0 moves away from Civic. The Prospectors' Notes and The Palace Archive are now Economic, so Tier 0 holds 1 Military, 4 Economic and 5 Civic seatable cards. (Since you cut The Palace Archive on 2026-10-21: 1, 3 and 5.)
  - Features A and B: yes.
  - Great cities may read town size: the Scientific player should play tall.
  - All 68 seatable cards stay. You cut by hand. (On 2026-10-21 you cut two and moved two to the shared cards, so 64 remain; see the last bullet.)
  - No charter opens a unit early (the ruling on the charter prototype, 2026-10-21, `docs/plans/scientific-themes.md` section 12). The Bronze-Founders', Mechanists', Alchemists' and Naval Gunners' Charters now improve a line of units the empire can already build. After the second ruling of the day (section 13), each line trait gives a small bonus and one ability, and the line keeps both through every upgrade. After section 14 the bonus is +1 at Tier 0, none at Tier II, +1 for the Grenadier (a reading to confirm) and +3 for the Galleass.
  - Your review comments (2026-10-21, section 14), marked "revised 2026-10-21 (section 14)" on each row they change:
    - Cut: The Palace Archive, The Fellowships, the Hermetic Charter and the Experimenters' Charter. They are in the last section.
    - Moved to the shared cards: The Assay (with Expansionist, new text) and The Companions (with Dominion, new text, proposed name The Soldier-Scholars).
    - Three charters open a new building instead of an old one early: the Glassmakers' (the Specularium), the Librarians' (the Mouseion) and the Printers' (the Aldine Press, keeping +1 trade route). The three buildings are proposals, to confirm.
    - New texts: the Physic Garden (+2 science, +2 happiness, one in each town), the Mechanists' traits (no strength), the Tophane (+4), the Grenadier (+1, to confirm), The Royal Estates, The Great Designs, The Edge of the Sword and The Strategikon.
    - The Second Seat stays (*"love this one"*).
- **Doctrines:** Scientific has none yet. They come after your cuts.
- **Cut cards:** the cards the themes pass cuts are in the last section, `## Cut by this pass`, with their sheet text and one line on why, so you can restore any of them. The ten live rows the sheet cut before this pass are listed under "Data notes".

**Data notes:**
- The Mechanists' Charter is Tier III in the data and Tier II on the sheet and in this list.
- Merchant Scholars is Tier I in the data and Tier III on the sheet and in this list.
- The Exchange of Discoveries is Tier III in the data. This list has it at Tier I, as the Commerce sheet does.
- None of the thirteen charter technologies is in `data/techs.json`.
- Buried seams (veins) are shelved: `veins.share` is 0 in `data/mapgen.json` (`docs/deprecated/veins.md`). The sheet's The Prospectors' Notes would pay nothing, so the themes pass reworks it.
- Ten live rows the sheet cut before this pass are still dealt: Practical Astronomy, Working Estates, The Endowed Provinces, The Royal Society, The Learned Chancery, The Academy Quarter, The Patent Rolls, Master Artificers, The Society of Artificers and The Learned Realm. Field Notebooks is live too, and this pass cuts it. The recommendation is to retire all eleven rows.
- Rows whose data text is older than the sheet: Learned Commissions (+38% toward empire buildings, and 60 culture and 30 faith for a first of a kind), The Assay (+3 science and +3 gold for each kind; the row moves to `common.json` as a shared card), The Quiet Faculties and The Long Inheritance (any great-person work, not Academies), and Merchant Scholars (+5 science on foreign routes from a Library city).
- Deferred rows this list keeps: the Glassmakers', Mechanists' and Alchemists' Charters, The Second Seat, The Strategic Reserves (now The School of Mines) and The Monastery Libraries. Deferred rows this pass cuts: the Metallurgists' and Naturalists' Charters, and Discoveries through Great People. The Hydraulic Commonwealth (`common.json`, deferred) is cut on both its sheets; retire it.
- The Scriptorium and the Orrery are building rows that exist. The charters that opened them (The Scriveners', The Stargazers') are retired rows in `data/statecraft.json`, so the new name The Paper-Makers' Charter avoids a clash. The Bastion is a row that exists with `awaitsTech`: no ordinary technology opens it. The Frigate and the Corvette are such rows too, but no charter opens them since the ruling of 2026-10-21.
- New rows the charters need: five buildings (the Bimaristan, the Tophane, the Specularium, the Mouseion and the Aldine Press) and three improvements (the Physic Garden, the Qanat and Niter Beds).
- The six line traits (Hoplite, Maryannu, Onager, Repeating Crossbow, Grenadier and Galleass) need no unit rows. Each is a set of effects on its charter technology, filtered to one line of units by a new `line` field, as Steel's +1 movement is filtered to melee units (`docs/plans/scientific-themes.md` section 3).
- Four sheet charters lose their "It opens …" clause (the Surveyors', Glassmakers', Mechanists' and Printers'), because no card depends on a charter technology (ruling (qqqqqq) 11) and most of the cards they named are cut. The Hermetic had one too, and you cut it (section 14).
