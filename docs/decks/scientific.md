# The Scientific deck

**What it rewards:** Reaching things first. The deck has fewer raw bonuses and a slower start than the others, and in return it gets units, buildings and improvements before their age, versions nobody else can build, and rewards for finishing a technology before any other empire. Its science goes into **charter technologies**, which cost more research than ordinary ones and open the deck's strongest cards. **Directions inside it:** charter research (draft a charter, research its technology, then draw the cards it opens); early access to units, buildings and works; being first; institutions that mature (libraries, universities, academies, empire buildings); strategic resources and the mine; scholars and academies.

*Initially generated by `scripts/deckDocs.mjs`; now a hand-edited design spec (deck-list pass, 2026-10-06). **Built** means the effect exists in the game as written. **Revised** means the card exists in the data and this sheet changes it (the last column says what it was). **New** is a card this sheet proposes. **Deferred** means the card is in the data and not implemented. Tiers are proposed availability, not confirmed runtime unlocks.*

**How the two steps work.** A charter is drafted, never sits in a chair, and opens its technology for research the moment it is picked (unchanged behaviour). The technology costs more research than an ordinary one of its age and will sit in its own section of the tree. A card marked **Needs <technology>** in the card column is dealt only to a seat that knows that technology. That gate is a new mechanic for every such card (*needs: a draft gate on a known technology*), on top of whatever the card itself needs. A gated card may sit in a later tier than its charter when it is too strong to arrive early.

### Charter technologies

Each charter opens one technology. The technology gives early access by itself, and it opens the cards listed beside it.

| Charter (tier) | Technology | What knowing the technology gives | Cards it opens (tier) |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Glassmakers' Charter (I) | Optics | The Observatory before The Astrolabe, needing a Library rather than a University | The Temple Observatory (I), The Spectacle Makers (II) |
| The Surveyors' Charter (I) | Surveying | The Aqueduct before Engineering; the Qanat, an improvement that waters the farms beside it away from a river | The Qanat Builders (I), The Field Survey (I) |
| The Schoolmen's Charter (I) | Scholasticism | The Cathedral and the Apostle before Theology | The Scriptoria (I), The Religious University (II) |
| The Metallurgists' Charter (II) | Alloys | The Crucible Guard, a unit only empires knowing Alloys can train; the Forge before Steel | Crucible Steel (II) |
| The Naturalists' Charter (II) | Taxonomy | +1 food on your worked plantations and pastures | New Crops (II), Husbandry Studies (II) |
| The Mechanists' Charter (II) | Mechanics | The Trebuchet and the Crossbowman before their technologies | The Siege College (II), The Engine Works (III) |
| The Printers' Charter (II) | Printing | The Printing House before Movable Type; +1 trade route | The Presses (III), The Encyclopédie (III) |
| The Alchemists' Charter (III) | Chemistry | The Fire Lance before Alchemy (Chemistry does not open the Magnum Opus) | The Saltpetre Works (III), The Apothecaries (III) |

None of these technologies exist in `data/techs.json` yet. A technology can already unlock units and buildings and carry card effects (Steel and Machinery do), so a charter does not need to unlock a unit itself. What is new: the technologies, a second way into an existing row (the Aqueduct, the Observatory and the others opened early), and the Qanat and Crucible Guard rows.

## Synergies with other decks

### Dominion — the military edge
- **Scientific gives Dominion** units early, or units nobody else has: the Crucible Guard (Alloys), the Trebuchet and Crossbowman a full age early (Mechanics), the Fire Lance before Alchemy (Chemistry), and siege units of the next age through a spent scholar.
- **Dominion gives Scientific** science from fighting and captured cities with their libraries and universities. Dominion takes ground while the expensive charter technologies come in, which covers Scientific's slow tempo.
- **Hooks this sheet creates:** units ahead of their age; extra strategic copies (The Ordnance Rolls, The Saltpetre Works). **Hooks this sheet reads:** kills (Copying the Enemy, Field Academies) and captured cities (The Carried-Off Scholars).
- **Shared improvements:** strategic resources on mines, pastures and camps. Scientific pays science and extra copies on them; Dominion builds its army on them.
- **Cards that serve the pair:** The Metallurgists' Charter, Crucible Steel, The Mechanists' Charter, The Siege College, The Alchemists' Charter, The Saltpetre Works, Husbandry Studies, Siege Engineers for Hire, Copying the Enemy, The Carried-Off Scholars, The Ordnance Rolls, Field Academies, The Engineer Corps.

### Expansionist — the engineers
- **Scientific gives Expansionist** improvements and public works ahead of their age: the Aqueduct and the Qanat (Surveying), and more food on plantations and pastures (Taxonomy).
- **Expansionist gives Scientific** the terrain and the population its science comes from, and growth that covers Scientific's slow tempo. This is the pair that covers a gap most directly: Expansionist's science.
- **Hooks this sheet creates:** improvements ahead of their age (Qanat, Aqueduct). **Hooks this sheet reads:** population (The Scribes' Hall, Hydraulic Schools), worked land (The Field Survey), stored food (The Grain Tallies, The Hydraulic Commonwealth), buried seams (The Prospectors' Notes).
- **Shared improvements:** mines and quarries, where Scientific pays science and Expansionist pays production; plantations and farms through New Crops (a farm beside a plantation, close to Expansionist's Three Sisters).
- **Cards that serve the pair:** The Surveyors' Charter, The Qanat Builders, The Field Survey, The Naturalists' Charter, New Crops, The Grain Tallies, The Scribes' Hall, Apprentice Engineers, The Prospectors' Notes, Hydraulic Schools, The Hydraulic Commonwealth.

### Commerce — the patrons
- **Scientific gives Commerce** trade unlocks ahead of their age: Printing gives an extra trade route and the Printing House early.
- **Commerce gives Scientific** gold that buys libraries and universities (covering Scientific's tempo), gold that buys research directly, and foreign routes that pay science.
- **Hooks this sheet creates:** Printing's extra route. **Hooks this sheet reads:** gold (The Presses), foreign routes (Books from the Ships, Merchant Scholars, The Merchant Navigators), Markets (The Exchange of Discoveries).
- **Shared improvements:** mines, quarries and plantations, the most overlap of any pair. Scientific pays science on them (Apprentice Engineers, The Physic Garden, New Crops, The Exchange of Discoveries); Commerce pays gold. The Assay lost its gold for this reason.
- **Exploration:** Commerce's theme, with its own cards there. Scientific keeps only Field Notebooks, for light overlap.
- **Cards that serve the pair:** The Printers' Charter, The Presses, Books from the Ships, Merchant Scholars, The Merchant Navigators, The Exchange of Discoveries, The Physic Garden, Field Notebooks.

### Devotion — scholars of the faith
- **Scientific gives Devotion** religious unlocks ahead of their age: the Cathedral and the Apostle before Theology (Scholasticism). Finishing technologies pays faith (The Celestial Rites).
- **Devotion gives Scientific** faith that buys science buildings and keeps research moving (The Scriptoria), and temples that teach.
- **Hooks this sheet creates:** early religious buildings and units; faith from completed technologies. **Hooks this sheet reads:** faith (The Scriptoria, The Cloister), Temples (The Temple Observatory, Cloister Schools), Holy Sites (The Religious University).
- **Shared improvements:** plantations (The Physic Garden, New Crops), and holy sites beside academies. The Religious University makes a Holy Site count as an Academy, so Scientific's academy cards pay on Devotion's work.
- **Cards that serve the pair:** The Schoolmen's Charter, The Scriptoria, The Religious University, The Glassmakers' Charter, The Temple Observatory, The Celestial Rites, Cloister Schools, The Cloister, The Monastery Libraries.

### Magisterial — the academy
- **Scientific gives Magisterial** charters, which are drafted and never take a chair, so they count as draft actions; and scholars and academies for Magisterial's any-family cards.
- **Magisterial gives Scientific** more drafts and rerolls, which means more charters seen and a better chance of finding the charter it needs.
- **Hooks this sheet creates:** charters drafted (counted by The Encyclopédie) and charter technologies known (The Great Catalogue); Academies that also count as Landmarks (The Museion). **Hooks this sheet reads:** passed drafts and extra cards in a draft (The Imperial Examinations), cards held seated or not (The Encyclopédie).
- **Shared improvements:** academies and landmarks (The Museion, The House of Wisdom).
- **Cards that serve the pair:** The Examination Halls, The Encyclopédie, The Museion, The Royal Charter Commission, Discoveries through Scholars, The House of Wisdom, Illuminated Treatises, and every charter.
