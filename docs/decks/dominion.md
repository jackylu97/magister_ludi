# The Dominion deck

**What it rewards:** An army that pays for itself. Training, keeping and using units sustains a working state, and peace, defence, raiding and conquest are different ways of using the same strength. **Directions inside it:** garrisons and military buildings as the civic base; training and fighting that teach; the barbarian frontier and raiding; conquest and holding provinces; unit quality and logistics; great generals and citadels; naval force; a won war that ends in reparations.

**Gap:** developing its own cities, because its production goes into the army. It plays around the gap in two ways: its military buildings pay civic yields, and conquest brings in cities other empires have already developed.

Government tiers here are proposed availability. The generator runs only on request and would overwrite these edits.*

**Review (2026-10-06).** A second pass that tested every card against the deck's themes and asked whether it changes a decision. Target: about 45 to 55 exclusive cards, shaped about 9 / 14 / 14 / 10 / 3 across Tiers 0 to IV. Before: 107 cards (12 / 28 / 41 / 24 / 2). After: 47 (8 / 13 / 15 / 9 / 2). The reconciliation of the six sheets (2026-10-06) made Mercenary Contracts, War Bonds and The New Code cards shared with their partners, cut this sheet's Copying the Enemy in favour of Scientific's version (now shared), and restored The Chartered Company and Carried-Off Scholars as shared cards. The balance pass (2026-10-06) made The Oriflamme, Convert It or Tax It and The Holy Army cards shared with Devotion, cut The Military Orders (the same placement as The Holy Army), restored Looted Art as a card shared with Magisterial, and changed the numbers on several cards. That leaves 44 exclusive cards (8 / 12 / 14 / 8 / 2) and 22 shared ones. The deck now has three arcs:
- **The garrison state:** Martial Law and The Standing Army, then The Soldier-Farmers and Stockpiles, then Standing Garrisons, Defensive Lines and Reserve Officers. Units kept at home pay their towns, whether the empire is at peace or on guard.
- **Military buildings as institutions:** Drill Manuals, The War Hall Schools, War Foundries and The General Staff, then Armoury Guilds and The Citadel Works, then Arsenals and the shared The Holy Army. Barracks, War Halls, Armouries and citadels pay science, culture, gold and production.
- **Raid and conquer:** the barbarian frontier (Blooded Spears, Blood Brothers, Commission: Clear the Frontier), then The Pacified Marches and Songs of the Fallen, then siege, Triumphs, Hegemony and War Reparations, then Total War and Commission: A Victor's Peace, then Imperium and Commission: Hold the Capitals.

**Tier 0 and Tier IV pass (2026-10-07):** added 3 Tier 0 and 13 Tier IV cards.

**User pass (2026-10-12):** Muster Rites raised to 6 faith and 6 food (now revised); The General Staff raised to +2 renown; Commission: Clear the Frontier's reward reworded; Drill Manuals moved from Tier 0 to Tier I; The Camp Chaplains added at Tier 0 as the deck's early faith. Shared: Frontier Garrisons cut (with Expansionist) and The Road Garrisons added at Tier II in its place; Rites of Passage kept; The Sacred Host loses its upkeep clause. 60 → 61 exclusive cards; 23 shared, unchanged (the sheet said 22 before this pass, but Professional Soldiers had already made it 23).

**Implementation (Dominion batch, 2026-10-12).** The sheet is in the game: 56 of the 61 cards are live and 5 are deferred (The Relay Stations, War Reparations, Commission: A Victor's Peace, The Legions' Labour, Circumvallation). Every Proposed cut that reached the data is retired there (kept for saves, dealt by nobody); Frontier Garrisons is a shared row and waited for the shared-cards batch, which retired it (batch S, 2026-10-12). Approximations are marked "Built as:" in the rows below. `test/sim/decks/dominion.test.ts` now checks the data against this sheet: the five tier tables card for card (name, rarity, tier, text), and the Proposed cuts against the retired rows.

> Light-touch pass. Dominion is one of the two reference decks, so built cards are kept unless they clearly fight the spec. The pass adds the three themes the spec added (unit logistics; great generals and citadels; war reparations), commissions, Dominion's improvement family (strategic resources, camps and pastures), and cards for the history lines in the pair entries.

## Synergies with other decks

Dominion's currency is units, spent on garrisons, raids and conquest. Its hooks for partners are captured cities, cleared camps, kills and battles won, reparations, garrisons, and great generals with their citadels. Its main improvement family is strategic resources (on mines, pastures and camps), camps and pastures. Cards this sheet recommends cutting are left out of the lists below.

### Dominion · Expansionist (the frontier)
- **Dominion gives** cleared ground: burnt barbarian camps and captured towns leave land to claim. **It gets back** levies (citizens turned into soldiers) and newly founded towns that need garrisons.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** cleared camps (Blooded Spears, Blood Brothers, The Pacified Marches, Commission: Clear the Frontier, The Barbarian Frontier) and captured towns (Triumphs, Hegemony). **Hooks it reads:** towns founded near a border (Marcher Lords), settlers trained at a Barracks (Military Colonies) and garrisoned towns on a road to the capital (The Road Garrisons). Veteran colonies (a unit retiring into a town) is left to Expansionist's own Veteran Colonies, since it pays the town in food, Expansionist's currency.
- **Shared improvements:** mines on strategic resources. Expansionist works them for production; Dominion counts them for its army (The Quartermasters). Pastures and camps sit on both families' edges through The Soldier-Farmers.
- **Cards serving the pair:** The Barbarian Frontier (shared, new), The Road Garrisons (shared, new), Military Colonies, Marcher Lords, The Soldier-Farmers, The Relay Stations, The Pacified Marches, The Quartermasters.

### Dominion · Commerce (merchants and their guards)
- **Dominion gives** protected trade routes, gold from naval raids, and reparations from defeated empires. **It gets back** purchases: gold buys the buildings an army has no production to spare for, and units in an emergency.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** reparations paid in gold (War Reparations, Commission: A Victor's Peace), naval kills paid in gold (Letters of Marque), guarded routes (The Escorted Roads), and Barracks that turn production into gold (War Foundries). **Hooks it reads:** a gold reserve (the shared War Bonds) and units bought with gold (the shared Mercenary Contracts and The Chartered Company).
- **Shared improvements:** mines on strategic resources: iron and copper are an army's metal for Dominion and a source of gold for Commerce.
- **Cards serving the pair:** The Escorted Roads, Letters of Marque, War Bonds, Mercenary Contracts, The Chartered Company, War Foundries, War Reparations, Commission: A Victor's Peace, Naval Supremacy.

### Dominion · Devotion (the holy war)
- **Dominion gives** spread by conquest: captured cities and battles won, with religious units travelling beside the army. **It gets back** targets (cities following Devotion's religion) and faith that buys and supplies the army.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** captured cities and battles won (every capture card on the sheet). **Hooks it reads:** cities following your religion (Convert It or Tax It, The Crusader Orders), religious units (The Oriflamme), holy sites beside citadels (The Holy Army) and faith purchases (The Sacred Host).
- **Shared improvements:** camps and pastures, including horses, camels and ivory (The Soldier-Farmers, The Quartermasters, The Horse Herds).
- **Cards serving the pair:** The Oriflamme, Convert It or Tax It and The Holy Army (all three shared since the balance pass), Muster Rites, The Camp Chaplains, The Sacred Host, Rites of Passage, The Crusader Orders.

### Dominion · Scientific (the military edge)
- **Dominion gives** science from fighting, and captured cities with their libraries and universities. **It gets back** units ahead of their age from Scientific's charters.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** science from kills and training (Songs of the Fallen, The Art of War, Field Academies) and captured cities that keep paying science (Conquered Archives). The shared Copying the Enemy and Carried-Off Scholars read these kills and captures.
- **Shared improvements:** strategic resources, all of them. Dominion builds its army on them (The Quartermasters); Scientific's charters unlock the units that use them.
- **Cards serving the pair:** Field Academies, The Engineer Corps, Copying the Enemy, Carried-Off Scholars, Drill Manuals, The War Hall Schools, Armoury Guilds, Conquered Archives, Songs of the Fallen, The Art of War, Siege Works, The Annexed Provinces. The "siege engineers for hire" line is carded on Scientific's sheet.

### Dominion · Magisterial (the triumph)
- **Dominion gives** great generals and the citadels they build, which Magisterial's any-family cards reward like any other work, and conquests that are moments worth marking. **It gets back** draft boons that fill an army's gaps and wonders that pay out across the empire.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** generals (Battle Honours, The General Staff, The Citadel Works, The Marshalate, Commission: Hold the Capitals, Commission: The Grand Campaign), citadels (The Citadel Works, The Marshalate), and captures, which the shared The New Code and Looted Art read. **Hooks it reads:** garrisoned towns with a Monument (Examinations of State) and Magisterial's on-draft boons (the shared The Sword of State, a unit on draft that counts as trained).
- **Shared improvements:** citadels, which are Dominion's fortress and one of Magisterial's works.
- **Cards serving the pair:** The Sword of State (shared, new; the pair's Tier I card), The General Staff, The Citadel Works, The New Code, Looted Art, Examinations of State, Triumphs, Commission: Hold the Capitals, The Marshalate, Commission: The Captured Marvel (captured wonders, the same reading Looted Art needs).

## Proposed revision (2026-10-13)

*A proposal for the user to cut, written after the first deck playtest (Mithridates, Dominion + Expansionist). Nothing here is built. The briefs are `docs/plans/deck-midgame-changes.md` sections B and C and the rulings in `docs/decks/deck-design.md` ("Card design principles", "Dominion is the tempo deck", "Only the current band is dealt"). This section must stay at the end of the sheet, after "Shared cards for the user to rule on": `test/sim/decks/dominion.test.ts` reads every table row between "## Tier 0" and "## Cards Dominion shares" as a live card, so tables placed there would fail it. Its headings say "Band", not "Tier", and its tables start with "Card name" or "Existing card", for the same reason.*

**The direction in brief.**
- Dominion's economy becomes **tempo**: one-off lumps and short bursts on moments the game already announces (a building finished, a military building finished, a unit trained, a town reaching a size, a government adopted, a war declared, a commission completed), plus two early flats that fade as the empire grows. None of the new cards pays more as the army grows or as time passes.
- **Most new cards are live at peace.** Of the 21 new cards, 17 pay without a war; band 0 gets six peacetime cards, so an opening Dominion hand is no longer half combat cards.
- The flat **civic riders on military buildings** (Drill Manuals, The War Hall Schools, Armoury Guilds, War Foundries, Arsenals) become faster training, faster building, or a lump when the building is finished.
- Dominion gains **its own levy and its own charter** (The Press Gang, The Keep Charter), so the levy and charter keywords appear in a Dominion hand even without Expansionist. It also gets two levy and commission readers and two more commissions.
- **Science and culture from fighting stay** as they are (Songs of the Fallen, The Art of War, Triumphs, Battle Honours).
- About half the existing cards carry two effects. Each one gets a one-line split or trim below.

### How the numbers were sized

- From `docs/plans/card-measurements-2026-10-13.md`: a seated card pays about 3% of the seat's yield. A seat makes about 55 yield a turn in turns 1–50, 325 in turns 51–100 and 1,050 in turns 101–150. Bands open at drafts 4 / 10 / 18 / 29, at about turns 30 / 70 / 114 / 159.
- One-off grants pay 0.39 yield per seat per turn today, too little to notice. The lumps below are sized to land at about a band's average while that band is being dealt: about 3–5 a turn for band 0, 10–30 for band 1 and 30–60 for band 2, counting how often the moment comes.
- The lumps deliberately **do not use `perAge`**. A flat 10 production is a third of a building in the first age and a rounding error in the fourth. That decline is the tempo profile the user asked for: strong now, fading later, and replaced by the current band's cards.
- Production costs from `data/rules.json`: an early small building costs 30–39 hammers and a medium one 40–52. An early military unit costs about 15–27 hammers after the +50% military surcharge.

### 5. Combinations this opens

Each combination works through a general moment or keyword, never through one card naming another.
- **The military building finished.** Raising the Standard (culture), the reworked Drill Manuals (science) and The Muster Ground (a training burst) all pay when a Barracks, wall, Stable, Armoury or Keep is finished. The Keep Charter adds one more building to finish. A seat that leans into it gets its draft, its research and its army from the same few builds.
- **Citizens into soldiers (with Expansionist).** The Press Gang or The Muster of the Fields spends a citizen, The Recruiting Sergeants makes the town's next units come out faster, and Coming of Age pays again when the town regrows to four. Expansionist's growth cards (The Barn-Raising, The Gleaners) refill the town.
- **The sprint into war.** The Accession, Commission: Raise the Host and The Call to Arms each hang a ten-turn production burst, and they stack. A seat that adopts a government, finishes its commission and declares in the same stretch raises an army well ahead of its neighbours, then Campaign Chests and The Victory Mint pay for the next push.
- *Small, with happiness as fuel:* Total War's −3 happiness per city is easier to carry with Expansionist's happiness cards (The Open Country, The Commons, The Grain Dole). This is a pairing, not a line of the deck.

### 6. New shapes this section needs

Four, each read by more than one card across the two proposals:
1. **`WindfallOccasion` `governmentAdopted`**, fired in `adoptGovernment` beside the existing announced occasion. Used by The Accession.
2. **`EmpireCondition` `beforeAge`** (`value: TechAge`), true while `highestAge` is below the value. Used by The Old Chieftaincy. There is no `not` composite, and this is a named member in the same way `atPeace` is.
3. **`WindfallOccasion` `commissionMet`**, fired where a commission pays its reward. Used by Victory Parades, The Grand Review and Expansionist's The Harvest Feast.
4. **`CountKind` `chartersHeld`** (Expansionist's The Charter Rolls). No Dominion card uses it, but The Keep Charter adds to its count.

Everything else uses shapes that exist today. Two points to check when building:
- The Recruiting Sergeants assumes `levy.after` effects can carry a `productionBonus`. `cardProduction` reads `liveCityEffects`, which includes `City.timed`, so it should.
- The Press Gang assumes `levy use: conscript` takes `minPopulation`, as The Corvée's `opus` use does.
