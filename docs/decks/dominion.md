# The Dominion deck

**What it rewards:** An army that pays for itself. Training, keeping and using units sustains a working state, and peace, defence, raiding and conquest are different ways of using the same strength. **Directions inside it:** garrisons and military buildings as the civic base; training and fighting that teach; the barbarian frontier and raiding; conquest and holding provinces; unit quality and logistics; great generals and citadels; naval force; a won war that ends in reparations.

**Gap:** developing its own cities, because its production goes into the army. It plays around the gap in two ways: its military buildings pay civic yields, and conquest brings in cities other empires have already developed.

*Initially generated by `scripts/deckDocs.mjs`; now a hand-edited design spec (deck-list pass, 2026-10-06). **Built** means the effect exists in the game as written, **revised** means this sheet changes a live effect, **new** means a card proposed in this pass, and **deferred** means the card is in the data but its effect is not implemented. Government tiers here are proposed availability. The generator runs only on request and would overwrite these edits.*

> Light-touch pass. Dominion is one of the two reference decks, so built cards are kept unless they clearly fight the spec. The pass adds the three themes the spec added (unit logistics; great generals and citadels; war reparations), commissions, Dominion's improvement family (strategic resources, camps and pastures), and cards for the history lines in the pair entries.

## Synergies with other decks

Dominion's currency is units, spent on garrisons, raids and conquest. Its hooks for partners are captured cities, cleared camps, kills and battles won, plunder and reparations, garrisons, and great generals with their citadels. Its main improvement family is strategic resources (on mines, pastures and camps), camps and pastures.

### Dominion · Expansionist (the frontier)
- **Dominion gives** cleared ground: burnt barbarian camps and captured towns leave land to claim. **It gets back** levies (citizens turned into soldiers) and newly founded towns that need garrisons.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** cleared camps (Blooded Spears, The Pacified Marches, Commission: Clear the Frontier), captured towns (Triumphs, Hegemony), garrisoned small towns (Frontier Garrisons). **Hooks it reads:** newly founded and connected cities (Soldier-Settlers, The Post Roads, The Military Roads, Marcher Lords), and citizens, which The Veteran Colonies adds to rather than spends.
- **Shared improvements:** mines on strategic resources. Expansionist works them for production; Dominion counts them for its army (The Quartermasters, The Iron Levy). Pastures sit on both families' edges through The Soldier-Farmers.
- **Cards serving the pair:** The Barbarian Frontier (shared, new), Frontier Garrisons, Military Colonies, Marcher Lords, Soldier-Settlers, The Soldier-Farmers, The Veteran Colonies, The Relay Stations, The Post Roads, The Military Roads, The Satraps.

### Dominion · Commerce (merchants and their guards)
- **Dominion gives** protected trade routes, plunder turned into gold, and reparations and tribute from defeated empires. **It gets back** purchases: gold buys the buildings an army has no production to spare for, and units in an emergency.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** plunder and reparations paid in gold (Privateers, War Reparations, The Tributaries, Letters of Marque), guarded routes (The Escorted Roads). **Hooks it reads:** gold reserves (War Bonds) and gold purchases of units (Mercenary Companies, Mercenary Contracts, The Chartered Company).
- **Shared improvements:** mines on strategic resources: iron and copper are an army's metal for Dominion and a source of gold for Commerce.
- **Cards serving the pair:** The Escorted Roads, Letters of Marque, Army Contracts, Mercenary Companies, Mercenary Contracts, The Chartered Company, War Bonds, Privateers, War Reparations, The Tributaries, Commission: A Victor's Peace.

### Dominion · Devotion (the holy war)
- **Dominion gives** spread by conquest: captured cities and battles won, with religious units travelling beside the army. **It gets back** targets (cities following Devotion's religion) and faith that heals and supplies the army.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** captured cities and battles won (every capture card on the sheet). **Hooks it reads:** cities following your religion (Convert It or Tax It, The Crusader Orders), religious units (The Oriflamme), holy sites (The Military Orders), rites (Rites of Arms).
- **Shared improvements:** camps and pastures, including horses, camels and ivory (Hunters' Offerings, The Remounts, The Soldier-Farmers).
- **Cards serving the pair:** The Oriflamme, Convert It or Tax It, The Military Orders, Rites of Arms, Hunters' Offerings, Muster Rites, Rites of Passage, The Sacred Host, The Crusader Orders, Scorched Earth.

### Dominion · Scientific (the military edge)
- **Dominion gives** science from fighting, and captured cities with their libraries and universities. **It gets back** units ahead of their age from Scientific's charters.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** science from kills and training (Songs of the Fallen, The Art of War, The War Chronicle, Field Academies), captured buildings (Conquered Archives, Spoils of Empire, Carried-Off Scholars). **Hooks it reads:** units you cannot build yet (Copying the Enemy), libraries and universities in captured cities (Schools for the Provinces).
- **Shared improvements:** strategic resources, all of them. Dominion builds its army on them (The Quartermasters, The Remounts, The Iron Levy); Scientific's charters unlock the units that use them.
- **Cards serving the pair:** Field Academies, The Engineer Corps, Drill Manuals, The War Hall Schools, Armoury Guilds, Conquered Archives, Spoils of Empire, Carried-Off Scholars, Copying the Enemy, Schools for the Provinces. The "siege engineers for hire" line (a great person who unlocks siege early) is not carded here; it reads more naturally as a Scientific charter.

### Dominion · Magisterial (the triumph)
- **Dominion gives** great generals and the citadels they build, which Magisterial's any-family cards reward like any other work, and conquests that are moments worth marking. **It gets back** draft boons that fill an army's gaps and wonders that pay out across the empire.
- **Hooks Dominion creates:** generals (The General Staff, The Warlord's Call, Triumphal Arches), citadels (The Citadel Works, The Military Orders, Warlords), and captures that trigger a draft (The New Code). **Hooks it reads:** great-person works and wonders in captured towns (Looted Art, Triumphal Return), monuments (Victory Steles, The Imperial Examinations, Examinations of State).
- **Shared improvements:** citadels, which are Dominion's fortress and one of Magisterial's works.
- **Cards serving the pair:** The General Staff, The Citadel Works, The Warlord's Call, Warlords, Triumphal Arches, Looted Art, The New Code, Triumphal Return, Victory Steles, The Imperial Examinations, Examinations of State, Commission: Hold the Capitals (its great general).
