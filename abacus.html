The Abacus — spike

The victory scoreboard as a thing on the table. One rod per player, one bead per point, slid left as it is earned and coloured by the family that earned it.

—

The object breathes on its own; Turntable spins it right round to check the silhouette from behind. Under prefers-reduced-motion the sway is off and the object stands still — the bead slide stays, because it answers a button you pressed. The clack is synthesised on the spot (two oscillators through a bandpass); no audio file ships with this page.
