## 8

Use case: stylized-concept. One landscape-format imaginative art direction for a civilization tabletop game: THE WOVEN WORLD. A surreal miniature world built from embroidery, tapestry, felt, carved wood and dyed thread. Rolling hills are densely stitched moss-green and mustard cloth, rivers are cobalt silk ribbons sewn into deep valleys, tiny cities are architectural embroideries rising into little three-dimensional felt and wooden towers. The map's geometric field divisions follow woven patterns, intricate but readable. Two oversized wooden puppet-like sovereign game pieces with expressive carved faces, extravagant asymmetric woven cloaks, one crimson and one indigo, stand on flat wood counters and tower over the tiny world. Small birds on delicate wire hover above forests of tufted thread. Illustrated physical cards have thick cream borders and tapestry-like flat pictures. Warm sienna, dark aubergine, dusty turquoise, chartreuse and cream. Artful stop-motion-film object photography, unexpected, sophisticated and charming, richly tactile fibres, strong silhouettes, warm raking light with deep cool shadows. Compact close three-quarter scene, no text or interface, no standard toy soldiers or generic castle backdrop.
