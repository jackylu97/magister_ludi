Controls

  • Left click a unit to select it — again to cycle a stack · a city to open it
  • Right click a tile to move there, an enemy to attack it — hover first for the odds. M then left click does both, for trackpads
  • A move ordered with no movement left is still taken: the whole route is stored, and the piece walks it at the start of next turn
  • Drag with either button to pan · wheel or pinch to zoom
  • T opens the star chart — click a star to research it, shift-click to queue it behind what you are already learning
  • Y yield glyphs · R resource marks · 12 pick a lens in the order the Lens menu lists them · 0 clears it
  • C Statecraft · H Religion · W Diplomacy · A the Abacus · V the Bead Race · ❧ in the bar keeps the chronicle
  • Buy Tiles on a city panel arms the price tags; click a tag to buy that hex. Select a trader and choose Start route: pick any available route in the Trade screen and the trader moves to the origin city and begins
  • Enter ends your turn, stopping first on anything left undone — an idle unit, a city building nothing, no research chosen, a draft unanswered. ShiftEnter ends it anyway
  • G toggles the hex grid
  • Esc backs out: move mode, then this panel, then the selection
  • The tutorial walks you through a first turn and explains each new thing once as you reach it. Switch it on or off on the opening screen, or ask for it again from ☰
