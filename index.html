Controls

  • Left click a unit to select it — again to cycle a stack
  • Left click a unit's badge to select it wherever it stands — even inside an open city's ring
  • Left click a city to open its panel
  • Left click anywhere else to deselect
  • Right click a tile to move the selected unit there
  • Right click an enemy to attack it — hover first for the odds
  • M then left click moves and attacks too — for trackpads
  • Drag with either button to pan · wheel or pinch to zoom
  • B founds a city with the selected settler
  • F fortifies the selected unit — defence grows while it holds
  • A worker spends one of its three charges to build a farm, mine or pasture — pick one from its Actions list, on the sheet at the right. Strategic resources need the right improvement before an empire may use them.
  • A soldier standing on somebody else's improvement can Pillage it from the same list: the works burn, you take the salvage in gold, and it costs a single point of movement
  • Y toggles the yield glyphs · R the resource icons · Lens in the bar for more
  • T — or the research card, top left — opens the star chart; click a star to research it
  • A — or Abacus in the bar — opens the score: one rod per player, one bead per point. No bead is earned until the Æras close
  • Left click any tile in an open city's ring to pin a citizen — a garrison does not block it; click its badge to select it instead
  • Enter ends your turn — it stops first on anything left undone: an idle unit, a city building nothing, no research chosen
  • ShiftEnter force-ends the turn, whatever is outstanding
  • G toggles the hex grid
  • Esc backs out: move mode, then this panel, then the selection
