About this pass

Generated terrain, hills, water and vegetation. Shallow, faceted banks, narrow edge rivers and level water. Painted light uses olive highlights, teal shadows and blue water with angular shallows, quiet ripples and sparse shoreline stones. Subtle Paper grain is attached to the terrain and props; Physical light uses the same models with conventional lighting. Biome colours blend in patches across joined land tiles. Sun shadows remain cached. Resources and discovery sites are modelled at their generated locations. Optional staged examples show a city, palisade, farms, pasture, mine, quarry, plantation, camp and fishing boat. These examples are not a saved game. Optional unit pieces show the first land-unit kit. Optional roads and borders demonstrate terrain placement; fog and gameplay unit states are not drawn yet. Finite inspection sheet; no visual wrap copies. This is a separate art preview, not the game renderer.
